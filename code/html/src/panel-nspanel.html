                Preview and editor

                

                    
                        Drag tiles, rows and page titles to change the order, drag chips from the palette to add
                        items and pages, drop on Trash to remove. Double click a tile or a page title to rename
                        it; the selector next to a title sets the page layout. A click on a tile or a page title
                        opens its settings beside the preview: what the tile does (switch, button, relay, scene, text,
                        thermostat, a page) and its topic, group or target. Changes go into the text above;
                        Save sends them to the device.
                    
                


                

                    
                        Pages as the OHpanel display splits them, updated while editing. A grid page's
                        "layout" ("4x2" default, "3x3", "3x2",
                        "2x2", "1x2"; "columns": 3 = "3x2") holds
                        8, 9, 6, 4 or 2 tiles per screen on the custom TFT (design d3731fbb); the stock TFT uses the
                        layout's columns of its 4x2 slots (6, 6, 4, 2). "per_screen" limits it,
                        "type": "list" pages are 7 rows per sheet. The preview follows the design the panel reports.
